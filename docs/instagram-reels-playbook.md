# Instagram Reels Playbook — Marketing Engineer / AI-GTM Operator

> Personal brand content system. Not part of the Brand Command Center build pipeline.
> Written 2026-08-08. Review at day 30, day 60, day 90.

---

## 0. The one-paragraph thesis

You are not a marketing creator. You are a **marketing engineer who publishes his receipts**. Every other person in this niche is teaching frameworks they read about; you are running a system that rebuilds itself every morning and measures things nobody else can measure — share of voice against Klaviyo and Omnisend, Reddit sentiment by theme, keyword decay, and how often an LLM cites your brand when nobody named it. That machine is not just your job. **It is a content generator that produces a publishable insight every single day, for free.** The entire strategy below is built on that unfair advantage: you don't source content ideas, you *harvest* them.

The second thesis: on Reels, you are not chasing views. You are chasing the right ~3,000 people. A marketing-engineering reel that gets 4,000 views and 60 DM shares from Heads of Growth is worth more than a 400k-view meme. Optimize for **sends**, not reach.

## 1. Positioning: the spine everything hangs off

### 1.1 The sentence

Pick one and do not deviate for 90 days:

> **"I build the systems marketing teams pretend they already have."**

Alternates, if you want a different flavour:
- "Marketing engineer. I turn GTM problems into software, on camera."
- "I don't write marketing decks. I ship marketing machines."
- "Growth operator who codes. AI-era GTM, built in public."

### 1.2 The wedge (this is the important part)

Do **not** position as "AI marketing" — that category is saturated, low-trust, and full of prompt-pack sellers. Position on the narrow, defensible, currently-unowned wedge you already have working code for:

> **Answer Engine Visibility / AI share-of-model measurement for commerce brands.**

You already have the hardest asset in this category: a live system that queries models on branded and unbranded prompts and computes citation rates. Almost nobody has that running in production. That is your beachhead. From the beachhead you expand into the broader "marketing engineering" identity.

Concentric circles:
1. **Core (owned):** AI visibility / share-of-model measurement
2. **Adjacent (credible):** competitive intelligence, share of voice, Reddit/community mining, SEO decay
3. **Outer (identity):** marketing engineering, vibe coding for marketers, AI GTM operating

Post ~50% core, ~30% adjacent, ~20% outer. The outer circle is what makes people follow you; the core is what makes them hire you.

### 1.3 The enemy

Every strong personal brand has an enemy. Yours:

- **The dashboard that nobody opens.** Reporting theatre. Decks that describe problems instead of systems that solve them.
- **The marketer who outsources thinking to an agency and a Looker template.**
- **"AI marketing" as a content genre** — prompt packs, wrappers, screenshot gurus.

Name the enemy in roughly 1 in 5 posts. It's the highest-share content you will make.

### 1.4 Proof assets (your unfair moat)

| Asset | What it proves | Content it generates |
|---|---|---|
| Brand Command Center (daily rebuild, self-contained, zero external requests) | You ship production software | Build-in-public, architecture, receipts |
| Share-of-model measurement (branded vs unbranded prompt citation rates) | You measure what others guess at | The strongest single content series you have |
| Reddit/community sentiment by theme | You mine primary sources at scale | Pain-point content, "what buyers actually say" |
| Keyword gain/loss tracking | You catch decay before it's a fire | Diagnostic teardowns |
| Competitor content-gap mapping | You do strategy with data | "Here's what your competitor is publishing that you're not" |

Every one of these is a series. See §5.

### 1.5 Brand consistency kit

Lock these on day 1 and never change them for 90 days. Consistency is not aesthetics — it's **recognition in a 0.4-second scroll**.

**Visual**
- One typeface for on-screen text (a heavy grotesk — pick it once, save as a preset)
- Two colours only: one high-contrast accent + near-black/near-white. No gradients, no stock motion graphics.
- Every reel opens on a **full-bleed text frame** with the hook in ≤7 words, 0.6s, then cut
- Series identifier in a fixed corner position (small, consistent, same place every time)
- Cover frames: uniform template so the grid reads as one system. Title on cover = searchable phrase, not a clever pun.

**Verbal**
- Sign-off phrase, same every time (3 words max). E.g. *"Build the machine."*
- Vocabulary you own: *share of model, answer engine, marketing engineer, the machine, receipts, decay, the gap*
- Banned words: *game-changer, unlock (as noun), leverage (as verb), hack, secret, insane, crazy*
- First person, present tense, short sentences. You did the thing; describe it.

**Persona guardrails**
- You are the *operator in the room*, not the guru on the stage. Show the mess.
- Never teach something you haven't run.
- Numbers or it didn't happen. Every claim gets a screen or a figure.

## 2. What actually drives distribution on Reels in 2026

Design the content around the ranking reality, not around what you wish worked.

**The signal hierarchy (highest → lowest weight):**
1. **Sends / DM shares per reach** — the dominant signal. Content that one professional forwards to another.
2. **Watch time & completion** (loops count heavily on short pieces)
3. **Saves**
4. **Comments** (especially replies-to-replies)
5. Likes (nearly irrelevant for distribution now)
6. Follows-from-post (weights heavily for *who* your future reach is)

**What this means concretely for you:**

- **Build for the forward.** Before you film, answer: *who DMs this to whom, and what does the sender look like for sending it?* If a Head of Growth would send it to their CMO with "we should check this," you have a hit. If nobody would forward it, it's a story, not a reel.
- **Front-load the payoff.** Do not build to a reveal at 0:45. Put the number in the first two seconds. "Brevo shows up in 91% of branded AI prompts and 45% of unbranded ones" *is* the hook.
- **Two lengths, nothing in between.**
  - **15–22s** for reach loops (one idea, one number, hard cut, loops seamlessly)
  - **60–90s** for authority (a real walkthrough that earns the time)
  - Avoid 30–45s — too long to loop, too short to be substantial.
- **First frame is a text frame, not your face.** Faces don't stop scrolls; claims do.
- **Captions burned in, always.** ~85% watch muted on first pass.
- **Use Trial Reels aggressively.** Post hook variants to non-followers only, see which one clears your baseline, then publish the winner properly. This is free A/B testing and it's the single most underused feature for accounts your size.
- **Original audio, low bed music.** Trending audio is irrelevant for this niche and actively cheapens authority content. Use a quiet consistent bed so your reels *sound* like yours.
- **Seasonal tailwind:** August → November is peak ecommerce-marketing attention (BFCM prep). Your niche's audience is at maximum intent right now. Weight Q3/Q4 toward commerce-adjacent angles and ride it.

## 3. Content pillars & the effort-tiering model

### 3.1 Five pillars

| # | Pillar | Share | Primary objective | Feels like |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Receipts** — proof of work from the machine | 30% | Consulting demand, authority | "Here's a number I pulled this morning" |
| 2 | **Teardowns** — diagnose a real brand/category | 25% | Reach + inbound leads | "Your competitor is beating you here" |
| 3 | **Frameworks** — how a marketing engineer thinks | 20% | Thought leadership, saves | "There are only 4 kinds of GTM data" |
| 4 | **Vibe Builds** — build a tool on camera | 15% | Community, webinar sales | "18 minutes, zero budget, working tool" |
| 5 | **Operator** — POV, career, contrarian, personal | 10% | Community bond, shares | "I got told I wasn't technical enough" |

Pillar 5 is small but non-negotiable. Pure-tactics accounts get followers who never buy. Pillar 5 is what converts followers into *your* people.

### 3.2 The three-tier effort model (this is your answer to "post more without more effort")

Do not try to make every reel good. Make **one thing genuinely excellent per week** and let it feed everything else.

| Tier | Volume | Time each | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| **HERO** | 1 / week | 3–4 hrs | The real thing. A build, a full teardown, a data investigation. Scripted, multi-shot, properly edited. This is the asset. |
| **HUB** | 2–3 / week | 30–45 min | One idea, one take, minimal edit. Framework, POV, a single chart explained. Filmed in batch. |
| **HYGIENE** | 3–4 / week | 5–12 min | Derived from the hero, or a raw screen recording + text. Near-zero marginal effort. |

**Weekly total: ~7 hours of effort → 6–8 reels.** That is the whole trick. You are not producing 7 pieces of content; you are producing 1 piece of content and 3 medium ones, then slicing.

### 3.3 The 1 → 9 repurposing ladder

Every HERO reel produces at minimum these nine artifacts. Build this as a checklist and run it every week without thinking:

1. **The hero reel** (60–90s) — Monday
2. **Hook cutdown** (15s, the single sharpest claim, hard loop) — reposted day 9 with a different cover
3. **The money moment** (8s, text-native, no face, the one number) — Thursday
4. **Silent screen version** — screen recording only, big captions, no VO. Different audience entirely.
5. **Carousel** of the framework/data — posts on a different day, indexes for search
6. **Story sequence** (5 frames + a poll + a "DM me X" sticker) — day of drop
7. **LinkedIn post** — the same insight in text form. You already have LinkedIn scheduling tooling; wire it.
8. **The comment-reply reel** — pick the best comment on the hero, answer it as its own reel. Highest-converting format you have and it costs 6 minutes.
9. **The offcut** — b-roll or a tangent you cut for time, posted raw as a "quick one"

Additionally: at day 45+, **repost your top performers**. A reel that hit at week 2 will hit again at week 8 with a new cover and a tightened hook. Roughly 20% of your posting slots should be re-cuts of proven winners. Almost nobody does this and it is free performance.

## 4. Idea sourcing: build a harvest, not a brainstorm

You should never sit down and think "what should I post." Ideas arrive from seven pipes.

### Pipe 1 — Your own machine (the big one)

**Recommendation: add a "Content Candidates" module to the Brand Command Center.** You already compute everything needed. On each daily rebuild, emit a small ranked list of publishable observations:

```
Content candidate rules (emit any that fire):
- any share-of-voice metric moves > 15% week-over-week
- any theme where Brevo/competitor gap widens or closes by > 10pts
- any Reddit thread in tracked subs with > 50 upvotes containing a pain phrase
- any keyword set with net loss > 25 in a week
- any unbranded-prompt citation rate change > 5pts
- any competitor publishes into a theme we score 0 on
- top 3 highest-sentiment-delta posts of the week
→ output: title, the number, the "so what", suggested hook, source link
```

That's a JSON blob per day and a card in the dashboard. It turns your job into a content assembly line. **Do this first — it makes everything else in this playbook cheap.**

### Pipe 2 — Reddit / community pain mining

You already scrape it. Weekly: pull the 20 highest-engagement posts across your tracked subs containing complaint language ("switched from", "cancelled", "anyone else", "is it just me", "help", "worth it?"). Every complaint is a hook, verbatim. Real buyer language always out-hooks marketer language.

Format: *"Someone posted this in r/emailmarketing yesterday. It's the most honest thing I've read about [X] all year."* → screenshot → your take.

### Pipe 3 — Answer-engine prompt mining

Your unbranded prompt set is a content goldmine. Every prompt you run against models is a video: *"I asked five models 'best email platform for a $2M Shopify store' 100 times. Here's the leaderboard."* Rotate the prompt, rotate the category, rotate the model. Effectively infinite, highly shareable, and it directly demonstrates the service you sell.

### Pipe 4 — Your own work log

Screen-record **by default**. Set up a hotkey and capture every build session, every dashboard walkthrough, every Claude Code session that produces something. You will not go back and recreate these; you will only ever use what you happened to capture. Storage is cheap, re-shooting is not.

Also: your git log is a content calendar. Every meaningful commit is potentially "here's what I built and why."

### Pipe 5 — Objections and DMs

Every question in your DMs, every objection in a sales call, every "but how would that work at scale?" — that's a reel. Keep a running note titled `objections.md`. These convert better than anything else because they're literally what buyers are stuck on.

### Pipe 6 — Format swiping (not idea swiping)

Keep a saved-folder in IG of ~30 reels whose *structure* works — pacing, cut rhythm, text placement, hook mechanics. Steal the skeleton, never the substance. Review monthly. Ideas come from your machine; forms come from the swipe file.

### Pipe 7 — Comment archaeology

Read the comments on competitors' and adjacent creators' posts. The top comment on someone else's video is a pre-validated hook — the audience already told you what resonated. Turn it into your own reel with a real answer.

### The weekly harvest ritual (45 minutes, Sunday)

1. Open the Content Candidates card → pull the top 5 (10 min)
2. Scan Reddit pain pull → pull 3 (10 min)
3. Read `objections.md` → pull 2 (5 min)
4. Write hooks for all 10 (15 min) — hooks only, no scripts
5. Drop into the Kanban `Idea` column, tag pillar + tier (5 min)

**The buffer rule:** never let the `Idea` column drop below 10 approved ideas. If it hits 9, harvest immediately. Running dry is the #1 cause of quitting.

## 5. Named series (this is what makes consistency cheap)

Named recurring series turn each post from a *creative decision* into a *template fill*. Launch with four; kill any that underperforms after 6 episodes.

**1. "Answer Engine Autopsy"** — weekly, HERO
Pick a brand or category. Run your prompt set. Report: does the model know them, does it recommend them unprompted, who does it recommend instead, and what the gap is worth. Verdict card at the end.
→ *This is your signature series and your consulting lead-gen engine.* It IS the sales pitch, delivered as free value.

**2. "The Receipt"** — 2–3× weekly, HYGIENE
15–20 seconds. One chart from the dashboard. One number. One "so what." No face required. Fastest content you own — this is what gets you to daily volume.

**3. "Vibe Build"** — weekly-ish, HERO/HUB
Build a working marketing tool on camera, timer visible. "Reddit sentiment scraper in 18 minutes." Take requests from comments. This is the direct feeder for your webinar — every episode ends with "I go deep on this in [webinar]."

**4. "Marketing Engineer's Notebook"** — 1–2× weekly, HUB
One concept, one framework, whiteboard or text-native. The thought-leadership pillar. Saves-optimized.

**Reserve for later (launch at day 45+):**
- **"Roast My Stack"** — audience submits their GTM stack, you critique. Pure community engine, generates its own supply.
- **"Ship or Skip"** — react to a new AI/martech tool in 20s. High volume, low effort, keeps you current.

## 6. Hook library

Hooks are the entire game. Write 3 hooks per idea, film the best, and use Trial Reels to test the other two. Keep this file open when you script.

### Archetype A — The number you have and nobody else does
- "I asked five AI models 'best email platform for Shopify' 500 times. Here's who they actually recommend."
- "This brand shows up in 91% of AI answers when you name it — and 45% when you don't. That gap is the whole business."
- "I scraped 4,000 Reddit posts about email marketing. The #1 complaint isn't pricing."
- "Your competitor gained 200 keywords last month. You lost 340. Nobody on your team noticed."
- "Three companies. One category. Here's who owns the conversation — and it's not who you think."
- "9.1% versus 33%. That's the entire competitive story of this category in one number."

### Archetype B — Contrarian / enemy
- "SEO isn't dying. It's being replaced by something you're not measuring."
- "Stop optimising for Google. Start optimising for the model."
- "Your competitor isn't outranking you. They're out-*citing* you."
- "Marketing dashboards are dead. Marketing machines aren't."
- "Most 'AI marketing' is a ChatGPT wrapper on a Google Doc and a $2k invoice."
- "If your GTM team can't ship software, you're outsourcing your moat."
- "You don't have a content problem. You have a distribution problem — and you've had it for two years."
- "'AI will replace marketers' is wrong. It replaces marketers who don't build."

### Archetype C — Identity callout
- "If you're a marketer who's scared of code — this is the only 60 seconds you need."
- "To the marketer who's been 'learning Python' for two years: stop. Do this instead."
- "There's a job title that doesn't exist yet and it's going to pay the most in your company."
- "You're not a growth marketer. You're a growth operator. Here's the difference and why it matters to your salary."

### Archetype D — Proof / receipt (screen-first, no face)
- "This dashboard rebuilds itself every morning at 6am. I haven't touched it in 40 days."
- "Nobody asked me to build this. It's now in the exec deck."
- "Watch me pull a full competitive intelligence report in 40 seconds."
- "Agencies charge $40k for this. I built it in a weekend. Here's the architecture."
- "One HTML file. No server. No login. Everything a brand team needs, and it costs nothing to run."

### Archetype E — Build-with-me
- "Give me a marketing problem in the comments. I'll build the tool on camera."
- "Reddit pain-point miner. Eighteen minutes. Timer starts now."
- "I'm going to build the thing your agency charges a retainer for. Stay for the last 10 seconds."

### Archetype F — Confession / story (pillar 5)
- "I got told my work 'wasn't technical enough.' So I shipped software instead of a deck."
- "This is the dashboard that got me in the room with the CMO."
- "I almost left marketing in 2023. One skill changed it."
- "The most expensive mistake I made as a growth operator cost a client six figures. Here it is."

### Archetype G — Prediction with a timestamp
- "By 2027 every serious GTM team will have a marketing engineer. Here's the job description, today."
- "Answer-engine optimisation is a category worth billions and literally nobody owns it yet."
- "In 18 months, 'share of model' will be a line item in every CMO's board deck. Screenshot this."

### Archetype H — Question that indicts
- "Do you know what ChatGPT says when someone asks for your category and doesn't name you? Because your competitor does."
- "Quick question: when did you last look at what people say about you on Reddit? Not reviews — Reddit."
- "What's your brand's citation rate? If you can't answer that in 10 seconds, you have a blind spot."

### Hook mechanics — the rules
- ≤ 9 words on the opening text frame. If it doesn't fit, it's not a hook.
- Specific number > round number. "91.7%" beats "over 90%."
- Name the stakes in the first sentence, not the topic.
- Never open with "Hey guys," "In this video," "Let's talk about," or your name.
- The first visual should be *the evidence* — a chart, a terminal, a screenshot — not you talking.
- Write the hook **before** you write the content. If you can't write a hook, the idea isn't ready.

## 7. Production system

### 7.1 Three reusable shot formats — that's it

Templating your formats is what makes volume survivable.

**Format 1 — "The Walkthrough"** (HERO)
Talking head medium shot → cut to screen recording → back to face for the verdict. Two camera setups, one location, fixed framing. 60–90s.

**Format 2 — "The Receipt"** (HYGIENE)
Screen recording only. Big burned-in caption. VO recorded straight into your phone. 15–20s. **You can make five of these in 40 minutes.**

**Format 3 — "The Notebook"** (HUB)
Text-native or whiteboard. Either you talking to camera with a full-screen text overlay building point by point, or a marker-on-paper flat lay. 30–60s.

### 7.2 Fixed setup (remove all decisions)

- One filming spot. Mark the floor. Same framing, same light, same distance, every time.
- Same shirt palette (3 shirts on rotation, all solid, none white, none busy).
- Phone on a tripod at eye level, 4K/30, lock exposure and focus manually.
- External mic or wired earbuds mic. Audio quality reads as credibility more than video does.
- Editing: pick **one** editor and one preset stack (auto-captions in your brand font, one transition, one bed track at ~-24dB). Never open a new template.

### 7.3 Scripting

Only HERO gets a full script. HUB gets a 3-bullet outline. HYGIENE gets a single sentence.

HERO script skeleton:
```
[0:00-0:02] HOOK — the number/claim, on a text frame
[0:02-0:08] STAKES — why this costs you money
[0:08-0:45] PROOF — the screen, the data, the build
[0:45-1:05] SO WHAT — what to do about it
[1:05-1:15] CTA — comment a keyword / follow line
```

### 7.4 Batch schedule

- **Sunday, 45 min** — harvest ideas + write hooks (§4)
- **Sunday, 2 hrs** — film the week: 1 hero + 3 hubs, back to back, same setup
- **Monday, 90 min** — edit hero, schedule the week
- **Wednesday, 45 min** — cut hygiene from hero, film 2–3 receipts, schedule
- **Daily, 30 min** — engagement block (see §9)

Total ~7 hrs/week. Protect the Sunday block like a client meeting.

### 7.5 Captions & on-post SEO

Instagram search and the recommendation system read your caption, your on-screen text, and your cover title. Treat every reel as an indexed document.

- First line of caption repeats the hook (it's what shows before "more")
- Then 2–4 short lines of context, then the CTA
- 3–5 hashtags max, specific not broad: `#marketingengineer #aigtm #ecommercemarketing #answerengineoptimization` — skip `#marketing`
- Put the searchable phrase in the on-screen cover text: "AI VISIBILITY" reads and indexes better than "the thing nobody measures"
- Alt text: fill it. Two seconds, real ranking value.

## 8. Cadence — the actual answer to "how much per day"

### Phase 1, days 1–30: **5 reels/week** (build the habit, find the format)
Do not start at 7. Starting at 7 and dropping to 3 is worse than starting at 5 and holding. In month 1 your goal is not growth — it's **20 reels of data** on what your audience responds to.

### Phase 2, days 31–60: **7 reels/week (1/day)**
By now you know which 2 formats work. Volume goes up because the winning formats are cheap to produce and the repurposing ladder is running.

### Phase 3, days 61–90: **7–9 reels/week**
Includes reposts of proven winners. This is steady state. **9/week is the ceiling** — beyond that quality collapses and the algorithm punishes it more than it rewards volume.

### The standing weekly grid

| Day | Slot | Tier | Pillar |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | 6–8pm | HERO | Answer Engine Autopsy / Vibe Build |
| Tue | 6–8pm | HYGIENE | The Receipt |
| Wed | 6–8pm | HUB | Notebook (framework) |
| Thu | 6–8pm | HYGIENE | Hook cutdown from Monday |
| Fri | 12–2pm | HUB | Teardown / contrarian POV |
| Sat | 10am–12pm | HYGIENE | Operator / personal / offcut |
| Sun | — | rest | Stories + community only |

Post at a consistent time. Your audience is B2B — weekday evenings and weekday lunch outperform weekends. Check your own insights at day 21 and adjust once, then leave it alone.

**Plus, daily:** 3–5 stories (behind the scenes, polls, DM prompts, reshare your reel with a personal note). Stories don't grow you; they convert and retain the people you already have. They are where consulting conversations actually start.

**Plus, weekly:** 1 carousel. Carousels still out-save reels and they index well. Turn each hero's framework into one.

### The non-negotiable rules
1. **Never miss twice.** One skipped day is a blip. Two is a new habit.
2. **Minimum viable post.** On a bad day, a 15-second screen recording with a caption still counts. Shipping something beats shipping nothing, always.
3. **Batch or die.** If you are filming on the day you post, the system has already failed.

## 9. Turning attention into pipeline

Distribution without a conversion path is a hobby. Wire this before you hit volume.

### 9.1 Profile as landing page
- **Name field:** `Farooq · Marketing Engineer` — the name field is searchable; put the keyword there, not just in the bio.
- **Bio:** positioning sentence + who it's for + one CTA. E.g.
  `I build the systems marketing teams pretend they have.` / `AI visibility + GTM engineering for commerce brands` / `↓ Free AI Visibility Scorecard`
- **Link:** one page, three doors — *Free scorecard* (top), *Webinar* (mid), *Work with me* (bottom).
- **Pinned reels (3):** best proof-of-work, best framework, best personal story. These are your homepage. Re-evaluate monthly.
- **Highlights:** `The Machine` (build in public) · `Autopsies` (case studies) · `Wins` (social proof) · `Work with me`

### 9.2 The CTA ladder — match ask to intent

Never put "book a call" on a cold reel. Ladder it:

| Content tier | CTA | Why |
|---|---|---|
| HYGIENE / high reach | "Follow for the rest of this series" | Cold audience, ask for the cheapest thing |
| HUB | "Comment SCORECARD and I'll send it" | Warm, triggers DM automation, drives comments (ranking signal) |
| HERO | "I break the full build down in the webinar — link in bio" | Earned the right to ask |
| Stories only | "DM me if you want this run on your brand" | Highest intent, lowest audience |

The keyword-comment mechanic is the workhorse: it drives comments (distribution), captures DMs (owned channel), and qualifies intent, all in one move. Set up DM automation for 2–3 keywords max — `SCORECARD`, `BUILD`, `AUTOPSY`.

### 9.3 The funnel

```
Reels (reach)
  → Profile visit
    → Free AI Visibility Scorecard (email capture, and it's a genuinely useful mini-version of your machine)
      → Webinar (the paid or lead-gen event — every Vibe Build episode feeds it)
        → Consulting / done-for-you Brand Command Center builds
```

The scorecard is important: it's a *product-shaped lead magnet*. Someone who runs their brand through it has just experienced the value of the thing you sell. That is a far shorter path to a consulting conversation than a PDF.

### 9.4 The engagement block (30 min/day, non-negotiable)

- **15 min before posting:** leave 10 substantive comments on posts by people your buyer follows. Not "great post" — actual takes with a number in them. This is how a small account borrows a big account's audience.
- **15 min after posting:** reply to *every* comment in the first hour, ideally with a question back. Comment velocity in hour one is a real distribution input, and reply threads count double.
- Reply to every DM within 24 hours, personally. Your first ten clients come from DMs, not from your link in bio.

## 10. Measurement — what to look at and what to ignore

### North star
**Sends per reach.** Target ≥ 1.0%. Above 2.5% is a genuine hit — repost that reel in 6 weeks and make three more like it.

### Weekly dashboard (10 min, Sunday, before the harvest)

| Metric | Target | What it tells you |
|---|---|---|
| Sends / reach | ≥1.0% | Is it forwardable — the #1 growth lever |
| 3-second retention | ≥60% | Is the hook working |
| Avg watch time / length | ≥50% (long) / ≥100% (short, looping) | Is the middle earning its runtime |
| Follows / reach | ≥0.5% | Does it convert strangers |
| Saves / reach | ≥1.0% | Is it reference-grade |
| Profile visits → link clicks | ≥8% | Is the bio doing its job |
| **Qualified conversations / week** | ≥2 by day 60 | The only metric that pays you |

Ignore: likes, follower count as a headline, view count in isolation.

### Decision rules
- **Kill a format** after 6 posts if median sends/reach < 0.4%
- **Scale a format** if 2 of 6 clear 2%: make it a named series, triple the volume
- **Rewrite the hook, not the content**, when retention at 3s is under 45% — the idea is probably fine
- **Repost anything in your top decile** at 6+ weeks with a new cover and a tightened first 2 seconds
- **Review formats monthly, positioning quarterly.** Don't change the spine because one week was flat.

- [ ] Ship the **Content Candidates module** in the Brand Command Center (§4, Pipe 1)
- [ ] Set up the Kanban (Idea → Scripted → Filmed → Edited → Scheduled → Posted → Analysed)
- [ ] Harvest 20 ideas, write 20 hooks
- [ ] Film the first batch: 5 reels
- [ ] Set up DM keyword automation for `SCORECARD`

### Days 8–30 — Find the format
- 5 reels/week, all four launch series in rotation
- Trial Reels on every hook you're unsure about
- Build the **AI Visibility Scorecard** lead magnet and put it in the link
- Day 21: first format review — kill the weakest series

## 13. Failure modes to pre-empt

| Trap | Why it kills accounts | The guard |
|---|---|---|
| Chasing viral | Wrong followers, dead pipeline | Judge by sends/reach and qualified conversations, never views |
| Perfectionism on hygiene | Volume collapses | HYGIENE gets ≤12 min, hard stop, ship it |
| Teaching-only content | Followers who never buy | Hold pillar 5 at 10% minimum |
| Changing positioning monthly | No recognition ever compounds | 90-day lock, review quarterly |
| No CTA / same CTA everywhere | Attention with nowhere to go | Ladder the ask to the tier (§9.2) |
| Filming on posting day | Guarantees missed days | Two-week buffer, always |
| Ignoring DMs | Your revenue is in DMs | 24-hour reply rule |
| Idea drought | Root cause of quitting | Buffer rule: never below 10 ideas |

## 14. The ten-minute-a-day version

If a week goes sideways, this is the floor. It still works.

1. Open the Content Candidates card
2. Screen-record one chart, 15 seconds, talk over it
3. Burn in a caption, post it
4. Reply to every comment for 10 minutes

One Receipt a day, forever, beats a perfect hero once a month. Consistency is the strategy; everything above is just how you make consistency cheap.

# Brevo Ecom — 25 Newsletter Placements Under $4k

**Prepared:** August 2026
**Constraint:** ≤ $3,000–$4,000 per placement
**Objective:** best ROI + audience relevance for Brevo Ecom (email/SMS/CRM + marketing
automation for Shopify / WooCommerce merchants, SMB → mid-market DTC)

---

## The one decision that drives ROI here

At a $3–4k ceiling you cannot buy the primary slot on a mega-list — Morning Brew's main
send is ~$50k, TLDR's flagship ~$15k, Lenny's is in the same bracket. Chasing those and
settling for a scrap of inventory is the worst version of this budget.

The higher-return play is to **own the primary slot on newsletters whose entire audience is
your buyer.** A 40k-subscriber list of Shopify operators converts better for an ESP than a
1M-subscriber general business list, and at this budget you can be the top ad rather than a
one-line classified. Every pick below is chosen so that $3–4k buys a *real* placement.

**Modelled unit economics at this budget** (from published 2026 benchmarks):

| Metric | Benchmark | Brevo Ecom at $3,000 / 50k opens |
|---|---|---|
| Effective CPM (on opens) | $40–80 B2B, $80–200 B2B SaaS/marketing | $60 |
| Sponsored-ad CTR | 1–3% | 1.5% → ~750 clicks |
| Effective CPC | — | ~$4.00 |
| Dedicated LP conversion | 15–35% (vs 1–3% to a homepage) | 20% → ~150 signups |
| Cost per qualified signup | ~$11–21 typical | **~$20** |

The single biggest lever is the landing page: benchmarks show a purpose-built LP converts
15–35% against 1–3% for generic homepage traffic. Sending newsletter traffic to
brevo.com/ecommerce with no campaign-specific page throws away roughly 10x of the spend.

## How to buy

**Go direct for Tier 1 and 2.** Marketplaces take a ~30% publisher commission (Paved's
published rate), which means a direct email usually gets you a better number for the same
inventory. Use Paved, Swapstack, beehiiv Ad Network or MediaPact for the long tail and for
price discovery on the smaller names.

**Negotiating levers at this budget:**
1. **Book 3–4 weeks out, or buy last-minute.** Publishers discount unsold inventory rather
   than run house ads. Stacked Marketer has said this explicitly.
2. **Buy 3 issues, not 1.** Newsletter ads underperform on a single insertion — reader
   recall needs frequency. A 3-issue commit typically gets 15–25% off rate card and is the
   difference between a real test and noise.
3. **Ask for the podcast bundle.** Ecommerce Fastlane, Future Commerce, Email Marketing
   Heroes and The Marketing Millennials all run shows. Host-read audio is usually thrown in
   cheap against a newsletter buy and outperforms the display unit.
4. **Push for CPA/hybrid.** The market is drifting from CPM to per-signup pricing (~$11–21
   per qualified signup is the current benchmark). Offer a lower base plus a per-signup
   bonus — publishers confident in their audience will take it, and it caps your downside.
5. **Ask for the segment.** Large networks (TLDR, Brew, Digiday) will quote the flagship by
   default. The vertical edition is a different rate card.

**Instrumentation — non-negotiable per placement:**
- Dedicated landing page per newsletter (not a shared one) — this is the 10x lever
- Unique UTM + unique promo code, so you can attribute self-serve signups that never click
- Post-signup source question, to catch the dark-social and word-of-mouth halo
- Measure to **activated store connected**, not signup — free-tier signups from Tier 4 will
  flatter a signup-only metric
